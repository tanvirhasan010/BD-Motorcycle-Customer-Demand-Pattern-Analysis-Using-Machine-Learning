The report reveals that the Bangladesh motorcycle wholesale market experienced strong long-term growth from FY2012 to FY2021, reaching a peak of over 603,000 units with a 6.3% CAGR, before facing post-pandemic volatility and contraction. The market structure has shifted significantly from Bajaj’s historical dominance to a highly competitive multi-brand environment led by Honda, Yamaha, and Suzuki. Seasonal demand patterns are strongly influenced by Eid festivals and monsoon cycles, with March–June identified as the peak sales period.

Machine learning analysis showed that the XGBoost forecasting model delivered the highest prediction accuracy (MAPE: 6.5%), outperforming Prophet and ARIMA models. The study also identified COVID-19 disruptions and post-Eid demand spikes as major market anomalies. Forecasting results suggest gradual market recovery over the next 12 months, with annual demand expected to stabilize around 450,000–500,000 units. The report recommends data-driven inventory planning, stronger dealer expansion, focus on the growing 125–150cc segment, and improved risk management strategies to handle future market shocks.
